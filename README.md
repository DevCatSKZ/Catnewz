## 🇩🇪 Deutsch

**Catnewz** ist eine Android-App, die Katzen-Nachrichten aus vielen Quellen sammelt
und automatisch nach Stimmung sortiert – **positiv, neutral, negativ**. Fünf Sprachen
(Deutsch, English, Español, Français, Italiano), fünf Ansichten, Werbe- und
Paywall-Erkennung, Lesemodus – **ohne Werbung, ohne Tracking, ohne Konto**.

## 🇬🇧 English

**Catnewz** is an Android app that collects cat news from many sources and
automatically sorts it by mood – **positive, neutral, negative**. Five languages
(German, English, Spanish, French, Italian), five views, ad and paywall detection,
reader mode – **no ads, no tracking, no account**.

### 📥 Download
- **[Download Catnewz 1.0 (APK) »](catnewz-1.0.apk)** · Android 8.0+ · approx. 3.7 MB
- Project page: **[Open website »](https://devcatskz.github.io/Catnewz/)**
- Privacy policy: **[privacy.html »](privacy.html)**

> Catnewz is not (yet) on the Google Play Store. To install, open the APK and allow
> installing "unknown apps" for your browser/file manager.

### 📺 Kodi
Catnewz is also available as a standalone Kodi add-on – no app or phone needed (Kodi 19–22; Raspberry Pi/LibreELEC, Windows, Android TV, Linux).
- **[Download the Kodi repository »](kodi/repository.catnewz-1.0.0.zip)**. With it, the add-on updates automatically.
- No download needed on the TV: add `https://devcatskz.github.io/Catnewz/kodi/` as a source in Kodi.
- Then *Install from zip file* → repository, then *Install from repository* → Catnewz Repository → Program add-ons → Catnewz.
